Hi.

January 5, 2024

Hi there. I’m Laura. And if you’re reading this, you’re likely a true Foxtopus fan and have really explored all the corners of our website.

Of course, there’s always the possibility that you just accidentally stumbled on it, too—in which case, allow me to make an introduction. Foxtopus is a teeny-tiny media company based out of Denver. I’m Laura; my partner is Scott and over the years, we’ve produced some award-winning and critically-acclaimed podcasts, published some bestselling audiobooks, and managed to survive (thrive, even!) in an industry that can be a little brutal.

This blog will be a space where both of us can jot down the ideas that have caught our fancy, tell you about the books we’re reading, share info about our projects (past, present & future), and help distract you from the things you actually need to get done. Chances are, you’ll hear from me more than from Scott but just consider any post from him a special bonus. And, of course, if you are familiar with our work, you know you can expect some weird things.

PS: If you’d rather get our most interesting posts in your inbox (especially the ones about Wild Thing), you can sign up for our newsletter below! It’s pretty sporadic but I plan to do more with it going forward.
