Is Bigfoot a conspiracy theory?

November 6, 2025

Did the FBI cover up the existence of Bigfoot? Or maybe it was the Forest Service? Those are just a couple of the conspiracy theories that came up during a conversation with John Sipher and Jerry O'Shea. The two are former, high-ranking CIA officers who’ve hung up their spy gear and now host the Mission Implausible podcast. You can hear the whole episode here and get my hot takes on Bigfoot erotica, as well as whether the government is hiding Bigfoot. There are also a few aliens and some nuclear power plants thrown in for good measure—and a bonus discussion about MK Ultra. Enjoy!
