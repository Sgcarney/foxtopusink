Quick answers

What is Foxtopus Ink?

Foxtopus Ink is the independent production company behind investigative journalist Scott Carney and science journalist Laura Krantz. Founded in 2014, it produces books, narrative podcasts — including Wild Thing, The Syndicate and Blood Vines — and longform documentary journalism.

Who founded Foxtopus Ink?

Foxtopus Ink was founded by married journalists Scott Carney, an investigative journalist and New York Times bestselling author, and Laura Krantz, a science journalist and host of the Wild Thing podcast.

What podcasts does Foxtopus Ink produce?

Wild Thing (hosted by Laura Krantz, three seasons — Bigfoot, Space Invaders and Going Nuclear), The Syndicate (hosted by Chris Walker, 10 episodes with Imperative Entertainment, on a Colorado marijuana black-market operation), and Blood Vines (hosted by Chris Walker, an 8-part series on a California wine-fraud case).

Where can I buy Foxtopus Ink merchandise?

Tees, stickers and the full Wild Thing collection are sold at the Foxtoshop, which connects to the Foxtopus Ink Shopify store.

What does the name "Foxtopus" mean?

Foxtopus is a fox-octopus hybrid — the company's logo and namesake. It's meant to suggest something sleek and clever, equally at home on land or at sea.
